// THE PEOPLE BEHIND IT

THE CREW

PHOTO
WRITER & GAME DESIGNER
JACEK KOMUDA
Founder · Writer & Game Designer

Writer, historian and screenwriter — one of Poland's most popular historical novelists. Specializes in the history of 17th-century Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth nobility. His bestselling series (Samozwaniec, Diabeł Łańcucki, Zawisza Czarny) have sold over 500,000 copies. Winner of the MKiDN Tomasz Merta Award "Between Literature and History" for lifetime achievement. Gaming is no stranger to him — he co-created the Dzikie Pola tabletop RPG and worked as a writer on The Witcher, Bulletstorm, Hellish Quart, Earth 2160, Aviators and Cyphers Game. His experience as editor at gaming magazines (Gamestar, Komputer Świat Gry) completes the profile of a creator who moves with equal ease between the pages of history and the screen.

PHOTO
DIRECTOR & VISUAL SUPERVISOR
MACIEJ JUREWICZ
Founder · Film Director & Visual Supervisor

Director, screenwriter and producer — Warsaw Film School graduate. Creator of award-winning short films, including the documentary "Dłonie", which gained recognition at several international festivals (Poznań, Banská Bystrica, Pisek). Screenwriter and co-director of the historical series "Diabeł Łańcucki" (2022) — an ambitious adaptation of Jacek Komuda's prose set in 17th-century Poland. His adventure with interactive storytelling began even earlier — as co-author of the Dzikie Pola tabletop RPG, which he created side by side with Komuda. He also brings his narrative experience to video games, having worked on The Witcher, Bulletstorm, Dead Island Riptide, Aviators and Cyphers Game.

PHOTO
CEO
PIOTR GOTKOWSKI
Founder · CEO

CEO at Basement Ideas. Classically trained musician and visual artist. Over 30 years as a freelance 3D modeler and animator in advertising productions. Previously Producer, Lead 3D Artist, Animator and Creative Director at Chronospace. Game credits include Aviators and Cyphers Game. A polymath who brings decades of cross-disciplinary creative experience to game development.
